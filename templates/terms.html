HackFest 6.0 CTF

⚡ RULES · TERMS · CONDITIONS ⚡

📋 Welcome to HackFest 6.0

HackFest 6.0 is a Capture The Flag (CTF) cybersecurity challenge designed to test participants' skills in reconnaissance, web security, analysis, and ethical hacking. By participating in this event, you agree to the following terms and conditions.

🎯 Rules of Engagement

1. Scope Limitation
Participants may only interact with systems, applications, domains, IP addresses, and services explicitly provided as part of the HackFest 6.0 challenge.

2. No Attacks on External Systems
Any attempt to attack, scan, exploit, or interfere with systems outside the challenge environment is strictly prohibited.

3. No Denial of Service
Participants must not perform:

  • DoS attacks
  • DDoS attacks
  • Resource exhaustion attacks
  • Network flooding
  • Any activity intended to disrupt challenge availability

4. No Sharing of Flags
Flags, solutions, walkthroughs, hints, credentials, or challenge answers must not be shared with other participants during the competition.

5. Account Responsibility
Participants are responsible for all activities performed using their accounts and credentials.

6. Respect the Infrastructure
Participants must not intentionally damage, delete, alter, or corrupt challenge infrastructure.

7. Fair Play
Use of publicly available tools is permitted. However, sabotaging other participants or interfering with their progress is prohibited.

8. Organizer Decisions
All decisions made by the organizers are final.

9. Right to Disqualify
The organizers reserve the right to disqualify any participant found violating these rules.

🔍 Challenge Information

Phase 1 – Reconnaissance
Your investigation begins from the provided endpoint.
Some information may be useful.
Some information may be misleading.
Carefully analyze every clue before proceeding.

Phase 2 – Misdirection
Not every path leads to success.
Participants will encounter:

  • False clues
  • Decoy files
  • Misdirecting information
  • Hidden hints

Careful enumeration and analysis are required.

Phase 3 – Final Challenge
The final stage combines information gathered throughout the previous phases.
Your objective is to:

  • Identify the intended vulnerability
  • Exploit it ethically within the challenge environment
  • Retrieve the final flag
